New projects get their first workflow block: `/foundation` interviews the founder about the product, the persona, goals and anti-goals, runs the `product-foundation` workflow to draft the document and find what is vague, puts those questions back to the founder, and writes `docs/product/FOUNDATION.md` only after a yes. Later UI, UX and product workflows read that file first. The same yes puts the product sentence at the top of the README, and `create` ends by pointing to `/foundation`.
